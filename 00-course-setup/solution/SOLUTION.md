# Solution: Course setup: what you need, and how this course works

`id` and `type` name and classify the case. `lead` is who is writing (name and channel). `turns` is the conversation, one string per message. `now` is the scenario clock, the input classical tests forget and Lesson 01 shows deciding a booking. `expect` is the full expected outcome: score, band, stage, tool sequence, refusals, escalations, flags, bookings, and reply count. If the field you could not explain was `flags`, it records side observations such as an ignored injection attempt, which Lesson 16 covers.
