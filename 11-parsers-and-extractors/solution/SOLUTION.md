# Solution: Parsers and extractors

The first number wins, so the budget parses as 500. The lead scores on a 500 budget (25 budget points) and routes a full band lower than its phase two money would earn. Whether that is acceptable is a product decision, but it is now a visible one: the pinned rule made the behaviour predictable, and anyone who wants first phase plus second phase summed, or the maximum taken, can change the rule and update exactly one pinned case to say so.
