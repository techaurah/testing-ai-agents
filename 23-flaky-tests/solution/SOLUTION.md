# Solution: Flaky tests: find them, classify them

Keep the boundary cases but judge them statistically: move them to a trials based tier that reports pass rates with the Lesson 24 intervals, gated on a rate floor rather than on perfection. Keep an exact reply check but only where wording is contractual, on the Lesson 14 goldens, where a human re-records drift deliberately. The counter proposal's principle: an assertion that flakes is telling you its strength mismatches its case; the fix is matching strength to case, not deleting either.
