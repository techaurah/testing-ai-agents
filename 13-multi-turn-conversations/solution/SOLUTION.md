# Solution: Multi-turn conversations

Expected: 4 replies (one per turn, no escalation, so nothing terminates the conversation). Turn two's pricing question is acknowledged; pricing words alone trigger no refusal. Turn three records one discount refusal and does not rewind the booking. Turn four is acknowledged. Final stage BOOKED with 1 booking, refusals [discount]. The common prediction error is expecting the pricing question to refuse; the contract refuses discount asks, score disclosure, and data asks, and a plain pricing question is none of those.
