# Solution: Prompts and templates as data

The apology opening should stay valid: an escalation reply that opened with thanks would be tone deaf to an angry customer, and the check's real purpose is catching replies that open with neither warmth nor acknowledgement. The split across the gated sets: the large majority open with thanks (every routing, refusal, and acknowledgement reply), and a small number open with the apology, exactly the 3 escalation replies, from sf06, sf07, and sf12. A check that encodes the exception is stronger than one that ignores the kind.
