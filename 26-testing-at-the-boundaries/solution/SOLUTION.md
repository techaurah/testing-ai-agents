# Solution: Testing at the boundaries: MCP tools and retrieval

Bridging connect to integrations (one synonym pair, applied at query time) puts d6 in q6's top 3: its overlap becomes 1 while other documents stay at 0 for that query, and no other query's ranking changes because none of them contains connect. Recall at 3 becomes 6 of 6, 1.0. The risk: synonym bridges are whack a mole that can also pull wrong documents into other queries' results as the table grows, which is why Course 5 measures bridges against a gap set every time one is added.
