# Solution: Anatomy of a scenario

A working example: lead on web, turn one carrying a budget of $900 and a pricing question (score lands WARM), turn two asking to book a call. Because the agent scores and routes on the first turn only, the booking ask on turn two is acknowledged, not booked: the stage stays QUALIFIED, no calendar tools appear, and the expectation's tool sequence is the qualification path plus one acknowledgement reply. The usual prediction error is expecting a booking; the first turn routing rule is the behaviour the fixture format forces you to state explicitly.
